Roman Reigns

Roman Reigns Leati Joseph Anoa'i (born May 25, 1985) is an American professional wrestler and former professional gridiron football player. He is currently signed to WWE, where he performs on the SmackDown brand under the ring name Roman Reigns. He is a member of the Anoa'i family, a renowned Samoan wrestling dynasty, and is the son of Sika Anoa'i and the cousin of Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson.
